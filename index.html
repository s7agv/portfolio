About me

Nasci no Brasil e me mudei para Atlanta, Georgia, em 2011, onde aprendi inglês e desenvolvi uma paixão duradoura por idiomas (também tenho certificação plena em espanhol). Desde criança passei bastante tempo em volta de computadores: brinquei com HTML e CSS, fiz mods de Minecraft e, na época do ensino médio, fui o único aluno do 3º ano da minha escola a concluir todos os cursos de ciência da computação oferecidos. Em 2016 estagiei em TI em uma cooperativa em Cascavel, PR. Depois do colégio (2021), trabalhei 6 anos em serviço, varejo e hospitalidade, sendo reconhecido pela dedicação. Voltei ao Brasil em 2025 para cursar Ciência da Computação e fui aprovado na PUC Minas.

Born in Brazil, I moved to Atlanta, Georgia in 2011, where I quickly picked up English, starting a lifelong passion for languages. At a young age I spent much of my time around computers and went on to start coding at age 10. Throughout elementary and middle school I often played around with HTML, making designs with CSS, and making mods for Minecraft. I spent the second semester of 2016 in Brazil, where I interned at a co-op in Cascavel, PR, under an IT position. In high school I excelled at language classes as well as computer science classes, eventually becoming the only junior in my high school to have finished all the computer science courses offered. I was also one of the very few students to obtain full accreditation in Spanish. During high school and ever since graduation in 2021 I spent 6 years working in the service, retail, and hospitality industries, where I was eagerly recognized for my work ethic and dedication. I moved back to Brazil in 2025 to pursue a degree in computer science, and after preparatory school was promptly accepted into PUC Minas Gerais, a nationally ranked, top-15 university in the computer science course.
